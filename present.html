USA · A word to leaders

Don't assume your church is every church.

  • You assume every church is like yours.
    false consensus
  • A healthy church assumes IT is the odd exception.
    imposter syndrome, the reverse
  • You seek out others who agree you're struggling.
    confirmation bias
  • The loudest, most visible churches feel like the whole story.
    availability
