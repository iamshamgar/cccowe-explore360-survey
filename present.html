The Usual Term

華人教會

  • “Chinese ethnic church”
  • 人 (rén) = people, ethnicity
  • Ethnicity as the label
  • Identity framed as a category
  • Carries inherited baggage
CCCOWE 2026 Explore360 · The Evolving Chinese Church Around the Globe© 2026 Enoch Y. LiaoTranslation
Enoch's Phrase

華源教會

  • 源 (yuán) = source, origin, wellspring
  • Heritage as lineage, not label
  • Inheritance that is stewarded
